## How to buy
1. Browse collections at /collections or search at /search
2. Select a product — sizes shown in EU
3. Choose size and add to cart
4. Checkout with Swiss or European payment method
5. Receive order with certificate of authenticity

- Model name (e.g. "Air Jordan 1 Retro High OG")
- Colorway (e.g. "Chicago", "Bred", "Shadow")
- Size (EU — convert from US/UK as needed)
- Condition: all items are new unless stated
- Price in CHF including VAT
